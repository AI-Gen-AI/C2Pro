Coherence Score v1 is now active for new C2Pro project audits created on or after 2026-05-01.

What changes:
- New scores use weighted finding severity, confidence, and project scope.
- Contract-only uploads no longer receive a default score. C2Pro withholds the score and creates an AUDIT_INCOMPLETE alert until the schedule and budget evidence are supplied.
- Historical v0 scores remain unchanged and are labelled as v0 in the dashboard.

Recommended action:
Upload the full contract, schedule, and budget triplet for each project before vendor review so the score can be calculated defensibly.
